# Validation Contract · v0.1 Candidate

## Gate 0 — mandatory research

Before report validation or Word generation:

- research plan and web ledger pass JSON Schema;
- plan, ledger and report identity/date/domain match;
- mandatory web research/query minimum is met;
- all selected sources are opened/read;
- search snippets are excluded from evidence;
- official policy source and policy-scope verification exist;
- current/future and primary-required claims have verified primary sources;
- secondary sources remain discovery-only for critical claims;
- all critical research questions are resolved;
- every report P/S/R source ID resolves to a ledger entry;
- every report standard has an official exact-name/version binding;
- no unresolved policy-date, policy-scope or standard-version conflict.

Any failure blocks DOCX generation.

## Gate 1 — report data

- domain identity, research assurance, policy scope, sources and analysis boundary;
- 20–40 unique concepts and 5–10 processes;
- concept use in relationships/processes;
- stakeholders, SoR systems, relations, events, decisions, rules, risks/controls, KPIs;
- standards with role, version/date, mapping, backbone judgment, strengths, gaps and local extension;
- shared-upper-ontology mapping;
- Agent capability, authorization, HITL, audit, review/appeal and A0–A4;
- at least three complete closed loops and all KSTAR stages.

## Gate 2 — Word and presentation

- complete Word architecture;
- research-gate summary in Chapter 2.4 and Appendix B;
- structural verification;
- render and page-by-page visual review;
- accessibility audit.

Human review remains mandatory for policy interpretation, T1/core concepts, R-Box reasons/thresholds, high-impact decision authority, professional correctness and any publication/compliance claim.
