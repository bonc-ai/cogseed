# Research Artifact Contract

## `research-plan.json`

Defines domain identity, research date, critical questions, accepted source classes, minimum evidence thresholds and conflict policy. It is a plan, not proof of completion.

## `web-research-ledger.json`

Records actual research execution:

- query log;
- opened source records;
- policy-scope verification;
- claim-to-source bindings;
- research-question resolutions;
- exact report-standard/version bindings;
- conflict register;
- completion record.

The `content_hash` is a SHA-256 fingerprint of the retrieved/extracted source representation. It supports reproducibility and change detection but does not prove source truth.

## `research-gate.json`

Generated by `scripts/validate_research_bundle.py`. It contains conditions, metrics, checks, blockers, warnings and input hashes. Do not author or edit `gate_status` manually.

## Synthetic fixture boundary

`research_mode=synthetic_fixture` may use synthetic URLs and evidence only for pipeline tests. Synthetic evidence must never be represented as real policy, standard validity, customer value, professional correctness or production readiness.
