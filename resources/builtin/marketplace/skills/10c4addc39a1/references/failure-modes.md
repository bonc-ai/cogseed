# Failure Modes · v0.1 Candidate

- `SCOPE_FAILURE`: wrong or unresolved policy/domain boundary.
- `MATERIAL_FAILURE`: missing, unreadable or incomplete internal source.
- `WEB_RESEARCH_FAILURE`: Web unavailable, selected official sources not opened, or query execution not recorded.
- `RESEARCH_GATE_FAILURE`: mandatory research bundle/gate failed; Word generation must not start.
- `SOURCE_VERSION_CONFLICT`: policy date, scope or standard version remains unresolved.
- `EVIDENCE_FAILURE`: unsupported claim, source binding or version assertion.
- `TBOX_FAILURE`: invalid, duplicate or layer-confused concept.
- `RBOX_FAILURE`: missing reason, source, priority or expert confirmation.
- `ABOX_FAILURE`: hypothetical/incomplete case represented as real evidence.
- `ALIGNMENT_FAILURE`: standard role/mapping/backbone judgment incomplete.
- `GOVERNANCE_FAILURE`: authorization/HITL/audit/review missing.
- `BUNDLE_FAILURE`: report-data invalid.
- `DOCX_FAILURE`: generation/verification failure.
- `LAYOUT_FAILURE`: clipping, overlap, broken table, missing glyph or blank page.
- `ACCESSIBILITY_FAILURE`: accessibility audit failure.
- `CITATION_FAILURE`: missing, dangling or misleading source reference.
- `TOOL_FAILURE`: runtime, web, file, renderer or dependency failure.
