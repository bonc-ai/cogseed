## AI Product Task Addendum

When AI or human-AI behavior is in scope, tasks must explicitly cover:

- Evidence provenance and source-type preservation.
- A0–A4 enforcement, confirmation points and rollback.
- Success, failure, refusal, empty and no-permission states.
- Eval fixtures created before implementation, including protection-surface negatives.
- Runtime/model/config trace capture and failure attribution.
- Submitted/Verified/Accepted status separation.
- Discovery/Authority/Release/Learning human Gate receipts.
- Change Candidate creation instead of automatic method or permission updates.

Do not mark a task complete on file presence alone; cite the test, receipt or human decision that satisfies it.
